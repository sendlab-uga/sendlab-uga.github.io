## Professionalism and appearance

Lab members are expected to dress and behave professionally at all times,
particularly during participant-facing hours.

## Absence notification

If you are unable to attend a scheduled shift, notify your direct graduate
student supervisor and Dr. Sanders as far in advance as possible — no later
than the start of your shift, except in genuine emergencies. Repeated
unexcused absences or last-minute cancellations may affect your evaluation
and continuation in the lab.

## Performance concerns and dismissal

If concerns arise about performance, conduct, or reliability, you'll be
given clear, direct feedback and a reasonable opportunity to address the
concern before any decision is made about continuation in the lab. In cases
of serious misconduct (e.g., breach of confidentiality, harassment,
falsification of data), dismissal may be immediate.
