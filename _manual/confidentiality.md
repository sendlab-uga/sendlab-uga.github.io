## Confidentiality and data security

Research assistants must protect the confidentiality of all study
participants and data in accordance with IRB requirements and applicable
privacy regulations. This includes:

- Not discussing participants or study details outside the lab
- Not accessing participant data outside of what's required for your
  assigned tasks
- Following all lab data handling and storage procedures

Breaches of confidentiality are treated as serious misconduct and may result
in immediate dismissal.
