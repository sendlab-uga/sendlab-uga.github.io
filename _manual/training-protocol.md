## Before you begin any work in the lab

Every research assistant must complete study-specific training before
beginning **any** work in the lab — this includes independent work and any
participant-facing tasks. Training must be kept current for the full
duration of your term.

*(This is a natural place to link out to session-running protocols,
scoring/data-entry guides, and any HBClab-style multi-session training
structure — trainer-led walkthrough, shadowing, paired practice, observed
session — if you want to formalize a step-by-step progression like that.)*
