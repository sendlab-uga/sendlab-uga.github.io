## Your first weeks in the lab

- [ ] Read this manual in full, including the [code of conduct](/manual/code-of-conduct/)
      and [confidentiality policy](/manual/confidentiality/).
- [ ] Sign the RA agreement / acknowledgment paperwork with your supervisor.
- [ ] Get added to lab communication channels (email list, Slack/Teams — ask
      your supervising graduate student).
- [ ] Complete required training modules (CITI/IRB human subjects training,
      any study-specific protocols).
- [ ] Meet with your direct graduate student supervisor to review your
      schedule and initial responsibilities.
- [ ] Begin the [training protocol](/manual/training-protocol/) for your
      assigned study before starting any independent work in the lab.

Training must be completed and kept current for the full duration of your
time as a research assistant — you cannot begin any work in the lab,
independent or otherwise, until training is finished.
