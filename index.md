## Welcome to the SEND Lab

The **Stress, Environment, and Neurodevelopment (SEND) Lab**, directed by
**Dr. Ashley Sanders** in the Department of Psychology at the University of
Georgia, studies how socioeconomic disadvantage, adversity, and the social
environment shape brain and behavioral development across childhood and
adolescence.

We use task-based and resting-state fMRI, DTI, structural MRI, and biological
markers (inflammatory and epigenetic) alongside large longitudinal datasets —
including our own cohorts and the ABCD Study — to understand these pathways.
